About

I’m Lloyd, a video editor in Manila. Since August 2026 I’ve edited paid social ads at Digital Rocket, a performance marketing agency, across 11 brands and accounts in e-commerce, legal, finance, health and franchising.

Before that I edited 17 YouTube Shorts for the MonsterBiker channel with Studio Oseven, built its visual style and an After Effects automation toolkit, and two of those Shorts passed 2.5 and 3.4 million views. I started out as a teacher, which is still how I approach a brief: make one point clearly, then make it stick.
